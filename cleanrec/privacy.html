Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CleanRec (the "App") handles your information. The App is developed and maintained by Lockrion.

1. Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

CleanRec is built to work 100% offline. There is no registration, no user accounts, no analytics, and no third-party tracking SDKs built into the App. We do not have access to your videos, photos, microphone audio, or any other files on your device.

2. Permissions

The App requests certain system permissions strictly to perform its core functions. These are requested only at the moment you initiate a recording:

  • MediaProjection (Screen Capture): Required to record your screen. The capture stream is processed locally on your device and saved directly to your local storage.
  • RECORD_AUDIO (Microphone): Optional. Required only if you choose to enable the microphone toggle to record voice-overs.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required to show the recording timer and the "Stop" button in your notification shade.

3. Storage and Security

All videos you record are saved locally in the Movies/CleanRec folder on your device. Because the App does not upload anything to the internet, your videos remain entirely under your control. If you delete the App, your recorded videos will remain in your device's local storage.

4. Children's Privacy

Since the App does not collect any personal information, it is safe for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at support@lockrion.com.
