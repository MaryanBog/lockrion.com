Project overview

Lockrion Reserve explores how a treasury on Solana can be presented through publicly observable blockchain data. The project brings together wallet balances, token holdings, and portfolio information in a reserve-monitoring interface.

On-chain visibility

Public wallet addresses allow observers to inspect balances and transaction history through a blockchain explorer.

Portfolio overview

The previous dashboard combined SOL and token balances with available market prices to display a portfolio overview.

Independent verification

A dashboard is a presentation layer. The underlying blockchain records can be inspected independently of the website.

Limits of reserve data

A wallet balance alone does not establish liabilities, ownership rights, or redemption guarantees. Market valuations depend on available price data and liquidity.
