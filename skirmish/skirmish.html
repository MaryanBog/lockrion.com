Hullhaven: Skirmish

A fast-paced card battle set in the Hullhaven universe for 2 to 5 players. Command fighters from 8 factions, empty your hand first, and convert card Power into Credits for the main game.

Download Game How to Play

Game Features

151 Unique Cards

The shared deck features 8 core factions (Humans, Synthetics, Arachnids, Crystallids, Thermids, Amphibians, Mycelids, Symbionts) with 16 fighters each, plus 23 Neutral creatures.

2–5 Player Matches

The active deck scales automatically based on player count, and a new Trump faction is drawn at the start of every round.

1 Power = 1 Credit

Every fighter carries a Power value. Points scored in each round convert directly into Credits, the in-game currency of Hullhaven.

Race to 5,000 Credits

The first player to reach 5,000 Credits at the end of a round wins the match and earns a Trophy 🏆 on their profile.

Quick Rules

1. Attack & Defense

The attacker plays 1 card. Defend with a strictly higher Power card of the same faction, a higher Power Neutral creature, or any Trump card. Trumps can only be beaten by higher Trumps.

2. Throw-In

Rivals may throw in clockwise up to 5 attacking cards per bout matching any faction already on the table. If you cannot defend, you take all cards on the table.

3. Empty Hand Victory

The first player to reach 0 cards wins the round immediately and scores the total Power of all cards remaining in rivals' hands.

4. Overload Loss

If a player ends up with 14 or more cards after taking the table, they lose the round with 0 Credits while all other players score the Power in their own hands.
