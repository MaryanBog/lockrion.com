Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 21, 2026 • Developer: Lockrion (Maryan Bogdanov)

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile card game Hullhaven: Skirmish (package name: com.hullhaven.skirmish), developed and published by Lockrion. We respect your privacy and are committed to providing a safe, offline-first gaming experience without tracking or intrusive data collection.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Hullhaven: Skirmish is a completely free, single-player offline game. We do not collect, transmit, store on external servers, or sell any personal data, including your name, email address, phone number, precise or approximate location, device identifiers, contacts, or usage analytics. The game does not require account registration or sign-in.

3. Local Storage on Your Device

To ensure proper gameplay functionality, the application stores minimal non-personal preferences locally on your device (such as earned match trophies, sound and music toggle states, language selection, and tutorial completion status). This data never leaves your device and is automatically deleted if you uninstall the application or clear its app data in your device settings.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Hullhaven: Skirmish contains no advertisements, no in-app purchases, and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

5. Children’s Privacy

Because the game does not collect any personal information from any users, it is safe for players of all ages and complies with applicable children's privacy regulations.

6. Changes & Contact Us

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect game updates or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. If you have any questions regarding privacy or support, please contact us at support@lockrion.com.
