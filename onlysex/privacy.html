Privacy Policy

OnlySex · Last updated: October 19, 2026

About this policy

This policy explains how the OnlySex mobile application by Lockrion handles information. OnlySex is intended for adults aged 18 and over.

Information used during gameplay

OnlySex processes your game setup and session choices locally on your device. These include player count, selected genders, animal totems, available accessories, intensity levels, and game progress.

This information is held temporarily in memory for the current session. The app does not save a permanent gameplay history or send this information to Lockrion or third parties.

No account or registration is required.

Device motion

OnlySex uses the device’s motion sensor to detect shaking on the task-reveal screen. Motion readings are processed locally to trigger the game action. They are not stored or transmitted.

You can also reveal a task by tapping the on-screen button.

Data collection and sharing

OnlySex does not transmit gameplay information, collect advertising identifiers, or send analytics or tracking events. We do not receive, sell, or share your gameplay data.

Support correspondence

If you email support@lockrion.com, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include. We use this information to respond to your request and investigate reported issues.

Email is handled through our email service provider. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information or details of private gameplay.

Support correspondence is retained for as long as needed to handle the request and any necessary follow-up. You may contact us to request its deletion.

Your information

We do not hold a gameplay profile or account record for you. Temporary game information is cleared when the app’s process ends.

For requests concerning information you provided through support correspondence, contact us using the address below.

External services

Visiting the Lockrion website, contacting us by email, or downloading the app through a distribution platform involves separate services. Those services may process technical or account information under their own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy if the app’s data practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions or support: support@lockrion.com
