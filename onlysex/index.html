Your game. Your pace.

Set the scene

Choose two or three players, pick your animal totems, and select the accessories you have available.

Choose your level

Pick from five intensity levels. You can change the level between turns. Any increase should be agreed by everyone.

Reveal a challenge

On your turn, shake the phone or tap to reveal a task. Timed challenges include a timer and an audible completion signal.

Keep it comfortable

Discuss boundaries before playing. Every challenge is optional, and anyone can stop at any time. A game rule never overrides consent.
